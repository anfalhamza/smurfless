Why SMuRFless MI Matters

Patients presenting with myocardial infarction may have no conventional modifiable cardiovascular risk factors. Understanding this population is important for improving recognition, prevention, and clinical care.

ABOUT

What is SMuRFless MI?

SMuRFless MI refers to myocardial infarction occurring in the absence of traditional modifiable cardiovascular risk factors, commonly including hypertension, diabetes, dyslipidemia, and smoking.

This platform aims to provide an accessible source for the latest research, evidence, and clinical developments related to SMuRFless myocardial infarction.
